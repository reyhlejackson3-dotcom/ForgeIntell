What is a Room?
When you purchase a system from ForgeIntellAI, your tools are hosted inside your own secure Room on our platform. Think of it like checking into a hotel — you get your own space, your own key, and everything runs independently. Your clients access their dashboards and tools through a unique room key only you control.
Systems without a website
If you don't purchase the one-time website build, your systems don't get integrated into your own website. They still work — customers access them through your Room on our platform instead. The $500 website option moves everything onto your own domain so it feels completely native to your brand.
Your key, your control
Every client gets a unique room key. You control who has access, and only ForgeIntellAI can change the key — so your tools are always secure and always yours as long as your account is active.
