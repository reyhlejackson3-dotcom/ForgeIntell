The Room

Where your systems live.

Every client gets their own secure Room on our platform — a private hub where their systems run, their data lives, and their tools are always on.

What is a Room?
When you purchase a system from ForgeIntellAI, your tools are hosted inside your own secure Room on our platform. Think of it like checking into a hotel — you get your own space, your own key, and everything runs independently. Your clients access their dashboards and tools through a unique room key that only you control.
Systems without a website
If you don't purchase the one-time website build, your systems still work — but they are hosted inside your Room on our platform rather than integrated directly into your own website. Your customers access them through a link we provide. The $500 website option moves everything onto your own domain so it looks and feels completely native to your brand with no ForgeIntellAI branding visible.
Your key, your control
Every client gets a unique room key. You control who has access to your tools and dashboards. Only ForgeIntellAI can change the key — meaning if you ever stop paying, access is suspended instantly. As long as your account is active, your Room and everything in it belongs entirely to you.
Always on. Always running.
Your Room never sleeps. Whether a customer submits an estimate at 2am or a contractor needs material quantities on a Saturday job site — your systems are live and delivering results around the clock without you doing anything.
